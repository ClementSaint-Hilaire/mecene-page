DISCOVER OUR SERVICES

From power distribution, cooling and process pipework to containment, fire protection and liquid cooling, every system is engineered for its interfaces, built in our factories, tested before packaging and delivered ready to integrate on site.

INTEGRATED
SKIDS

Prefabricated mechanical skids that bring pumps, valves, pipework and connections together on one frame, for cooling plant, water treatment and distribution. Built in our factories and delivered as one unit, ready to connect.

INSTRUMENTATION
& CONTROL

Prefabricated mechanical skids that bring pumps, valves, pipework, instrumentation, control and connections together on one frame, for cooling plant, water treatment and distribution. Built in our factories and delivered as one unit, ready to connect.

HOT AISLE
CONTAINMENT

Containment systems that enclose the hot aisle behind server racks, with roof panels, doors and return ducting that carry exhaust air back to the cooling units without mixing. Built in our factories around each room layout and delivered ready to install.

SUPPORT STRUCTURES

Steel support structures carrying pipework, busway, cable trays and containment, designed for load, transport and site assembly, with seismic compliance where required.

LOW-VOLTAGE
SYSTEMS

Filler panels with pre-positioned low-voltage safety and service components: cable containment, fibre routes, lighting and emergency lighting, reducing installation hours and coordination between trades on site.

COOLING AND
PROCESS PIPEWORK

Prefabricated pipework systems including headers, branches, valves, supports and bolted interfaces, designed to minimise field welding and speed up installation.

AISLE CONTAINMENT
& CROSS-AISLES

Containment systems designed to separate hot and cold airflow within Data Center environments. Aisle containment and cross-aisle configurations help organise airflow paths around server racks, taking account of room layouts and the facility’s cooling requirements. Cross-aisles can integrate cameras, sensors and auxiliary services.

POWER
DISTRIBUTION

Integrated busway levels, tap-off boxes, transfer equipment, cabling and supports, assembled and checked before shipment.

LIQUID
COOLING

Liquid cooling systems for high-density computing, built around the CDU of the client's chosen OEM or a qualified CDU sourced through our OEM partners: secondary loop piping, manifolds, supports and flushing arrangements, prefabricated in our factories and configured to each project's technical and installation requirements.

FIRE
PROTECTION

Prefabricated fire protection pipework, including sprinkler mains, branches, drops and supports, built to each project's layout, assembled and checked in our factories before shipment, ready to connect on site.

F.A.Q

Discover Verdon’s expertise in engineering, manufacturing and Data Center infrastructure.

Verdon combines engineering expertise and industrial manufacturing to develop and produce technical systems for complex industrial environments. With a strong focus on Data Centers, the company brings together technical design, manufacturing processes and system integration to address the requirements of demanding infrastructure projects.

Verdon supports Data Center projects through the engineering and off-site manufacturing of technical systems designed for complex MEP infrastructure. Its approach takes into account equipment interfaces, installation constraints and the coordination required to integrate systems into large-scale facilities.

Verdon operates across four key sectors: Data Centers, Energy, Life Sciences & Pharmaceuticals, and Semiconductors. Each sector presents specific technical requirements, operating conditions and infrastructure constraints. Verdon applies its engineering and manufacturing capabilities to address the needs of these different industrial environments.

Verdon’s capabilities cover a range of technical systems, including electrical infrastructure, cooling pipework, aisle containment, fire protection, liquid cooling assemblies and associated support structures. Each system addresses specific functional requirements and equipment interfaces, with its configuration defined according to project specifications.

Verdon brings engineering and manufacturing capabilities together to prepare technical systems for integration into complex facilities. By considering technical specifications, equipment interfaces and manufacturing constraints, the company works to align system design with practical installation requirements from the outset.

Verdon develops technical systems around the specifications and constraints of each project. Equipment configurations, spatial limitations, connection interfaces and installation requirements inform the approach taken. This allows the scope and configuration of each solution to be defined in line with the project’s technical needs.
