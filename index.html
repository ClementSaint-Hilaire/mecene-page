DISCOVER OUR SERVICES

VERDON’s systems and solutions cover key technical infrastructures, from electrical distribution and cooling networks to airflow containment and liquid cooling assemblies. Each system addresses specific functional requirements, equipment interfaces and integration constraints within complex industrial environments.

INTEGRATED
SKIDS

Integrated skid assemblies bring selected liquid cooling components together on a common supporting frame. Each assembly can be configured around equipment layouts, pipework connections and project requirements, providing a consolidated unit for installation within the wider cooling system.

SUPPORT STRUCTURES

Structural frameworks designed to support liquid cooling equipment, pipework and associated components. Their configuration takes into account equipment interfaces, spatial constraints and installation layouts, helping accommodate the technical requirements of each cooling system.

LOW-VOLTAGE
SYSTEMS

Low-voltage electrical systems designed to meet the technical requirements of complex industrial environments. VERDON supports the preparation and integration of electrical equipment, with configurations adapted to project specifications and installation constraints.

COOLING
PIPEWORK

Cooling pipework designed to support the thermal management requirements of demanding industrial facilities. Each system is configured around project-specific layouts, piping connections and equipment interfaces to facilitate integration within the wider cooling infrastructure.

AISLE CONTAINMENT
& CROSS-AISLES

Containment systems designed to separate hot and cold airflow within Data Center environments. Aisle containment and cross-aisle configurations help organise airflow paths around server racks, taking account of room layouts and the facility’s cooling requirements.

POWER
DISTRIBUTION

Electrical distribution systems designed to organise and route power across technical installations. Solutions are configured around equipment specifications, distribution requirements and connection interfaces to support integration into the wider electrical infrastructure.

LIQUID
COOLING

Liquid cooling solutions designed to meet the thermal demands of high-density computing environments. The offering includes integrated skids and dedicated support structures, bringing together key elements of the cooling infrastructure according to project-specific technical and installation requirements.

Discover VERDON’s expertise in engineering, manufacturing and Data Center infrastructure.

VERDON combines engineering expertise and industrial manufacturing to develop and produce technical systems for complex industrial environments. With a strong focus on Data Centers, the company brings together technical design, manufacturing processes and system integration to address the requirements of demanding infrastructure projects.

VERDON supports Data Center projects through the engineering and off-site manufacturing of technical systems designed for complex MEP infrastructure. Its approach takes into account equipment interfaces, installation constraints and the coordination required to integrate systems into large-scale facilities.

VERDON operates across four key sectors: Data Centers, Energy, Life Sciences & Pharmaceuticals, and Semiconductors. Each sector presents specific technical requirements, operating conditions and infrastructure constraints. VERDON applies its engineering and manufacturing capabilities to address the needs of these different industrial environments.

VERDON’s capabilities cover a range of technical systems, including electrical infrastructure, cooling pipework, aisle containment, liquid cooling assemblies and associated support structures. Each system addresses specific functional requirements and equipment interfaces, with its configuration defined according to project specifications.

VERDON brings engineering and manufacturing capabilities together to prepare technical systems for integration into complex facilities. By considering technical specifications, equipment interfaces and manufacturing constraints, the company works to align system design with practical installation requirements from the outset.

VERDON develops technical systems around the specifications and constraints of each project. Equipment configurations, spatial limitations, connection interfaces and installation requirements inform the approach taken. This allows the scope and configuration of each solution to be defined in line with the project’s technical needs.
